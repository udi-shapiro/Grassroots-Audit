\section{Status}

For each published report, the table records the GLP commit, report date, number of findings and number without a project answer. It is generated by \dir{bin/status.py}. The \emph{Unanswered} column does not count answers awaiting verification or disagreements after an answer; those states are recorded under the findings.

Coverage is stated in each report. The absence of a report means that no audit has been published; the absence of findings does not establish coverage beyond the stated examination. The \dir{GPT-Auditor} branch records assignments, work in progress, pending exchanges, dependencies and session handovers.

The public \dir{ISSUES.md} index links all numbered findings to their GitHub issues, lists their current disposition and identifies the work needed for closure. As of 25 November 2026 the 32 reports contain 50 numbered findings. Eighteen are resolved, including withdrawn claims. Of the 32 open findings, 27 have answers and five await a first answer. The Villagers review verifies 57 catalogue contracts and records five findings about the paper, its seven proposed unions and their constituents. All 91 contracts reproduce from their published inputs. Two generated terms change the decision role of a ward. The five-page Calculus revision resolves six of the eight previous paper findings. Its two main proof claims and the reflexive-lift case remain open. All six original core findings have project answers as of 17 November 2026; the answer-based hold has ended and independent verification is pending.
