\section{Status}

For each published report, the table records the GLP commit, report date, number of findings and number without a project answer. It is generated by \dir{bin/status.py}. The \emph{Unanswered} column does not count answers awaiting verification or disagreements after an answer; those states are recorded under the findings.

Coverage is stated in each report. The absence of a report means that no audit has been published; the absence of findings does not establish coverage beyond the stated examination. The \dir{GPT-Auditor} branch records assignments, work in progress, pending exchanges, dependencies and session handovers.

The public \dir{ISSUES.md} index links all numbered findings to their GitHub issues, lists their current disposition and identifies the work needed for closure. As of 24 November 2026 the 29 reports contain 33 numbered findings. Of the 23 unresolved findings, 15 have project answers and eight await their first answer. Ten findings have independently verified corrections. The latest Calculus review concerns the revised paper and verifies the wording of the Agents example. All six original core findings have project answers as of 17 November 2026; the answer-based hold has ended and verification of the remaining core findings continues.
