\section{Status}

For each published report, the table records the GLP commit, report date, number of findings and number without a project answer. It is generated by \dir{bin/status.py}. The \emph{Unanswered} column does not count answers awaiting verification or disagreements after an answer; those states are recorded under the findings.

Coverage is stated in each report. The absence of a report means that no audit has been published; the absence of findings does not establish coverage beyond the stated examination. The \dir{GPT-Auditor} branch records assignments, work in progress, pending exchanges, dependencies and session handovers.
