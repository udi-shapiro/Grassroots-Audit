\subsection{The project's published structure}

The roster below follows the Projects page at \url{https://peoplesweb.org/#projects}, as defined by \dir{PROJECTS} and \dir{tree} in \dir{servers/peoplesweb/tools/directories.py}. This snapshot is from GLP commit \dir{56ef162910cf}, read on 12 November 2026. The paper keys are those of \dir{tools/papers.py} in the same website source directory.

Each listed project has one GPT auditor covering its papers and code together. Under a project are its papers; under a paper are its directories and contracts; under a contract are its text or own directory, app, phone file and cascade record where present. Directories shared by several papers of the same project stay beside those papers. Reports retain the actual GLP paths of these artifacts.

Thus the current source names 20 projects holding 27 papers and, under Code, seven additional responsible projects: GPW, GCE, GFWC, P99, Phones, Coordination and Code. Currencies already has an auditor in the first table. These are assignments to establish, not a claim that the sessions are active.

The Papers page also lists 11 \emph{Earlier papers} outside the project roster. They remain in the inventory without an invented project assignment. Coordination records their pending audit responsibility. Contracts or source records that refer to additional material retain those references; their displayed position is not silently changed.

Since 12 November 2026 the website, \dir{servers/peoplesweb}, is the responsibility of a project of its own, Website, split from GSA; it has no paper and will appear under \emph{Code} once the site's record is updated, and it has an auditor of its own from then on.

The \dir{GPT-Auditor} branch records the complete project, paper, contract and artifact inventory and its source version. On a structural change, coordination compares the published map, updates the corresponding assignments and records the handover. Changes in ownership do not erase earlier findings or audit coverage.
