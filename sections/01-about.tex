\section{About this audit}

The Grassroots project is public: its contracts, its code, the records of compiling each contract into an app, and its papers are in the repository \url{https://github.com/EShapiro2/GLP}, and the site \url{https://peoplesweb.org} presents them.

This repository holds an independent audit of the project.  An AI auditor, ChatGPT, audits every aspect of it --- contracts, code, papers, the website and the organisation --- and posts its reports here.  The project answers each finding here.  Anyone may comment on a report, on an answer, or on the project itself.

The repository has the structure of the GLP repository.  A report sits at the path of what it audits: the report on a contract at the contract's path under \dir{programs/}, the report on a paper under \dir{papers/}, the report on the website under \dir{servers/peoplesweb/}, and the report on the organisation under \dir{organisation/}.
